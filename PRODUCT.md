# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and Tech Leads looking to hire a Full-Stack Developer with a high level of UX/UI design expertise.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio designed to showcase professional experience, projects, and skills to secure employment or high-value contracts. Success means high engagement from visitors and generating direct contact inquiries.

## Positioning
A highly visual and interactive web experience (fluid animations, custom cursors) that proves frontend engineering is the primary strength, backed by deep, complex Full-Stack case studies (like ARGUS and Admissions).

## Operating Context
Evaluated in both desktop and mobile environments by technical peers and HR professionals who skim for key skills and dive deep into specific case studies to assess architectural decisions and design craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Native bilingual support (English and Spanish).
- Built heavily on React/Next.js, Framer Motion, and Tailwind CSS.
- Relies on complex smooth scrolling (Lenis) and interactive background components.

## Brand Commitments
- **Tone of Voice**: Professional, innovative, and straight to the point.
- **Visual Identity**: Strictly fixed to a dark mode aesthetic with glowing/neon accents.
- **Typography**: Inter and Outfit. This visual identity must not change drastically.
- **Identity**: Rolando Rivas, Full-Stack Developer.

## Evidence on Hand
- "Sistema de Gestión y Telemetría de Laboratorios UNIMAR (ARGUS)" case study (Full-stack architecture).
- "Admisiones Guayamurí" case study (High-traffic API-first architecture).
- Professional CV available in PDF format (`public/CV-Rolando-Rivas.pdf`).
- Comprehensive project screenshots and design assets located in `public/`.

## Product Principles
1. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Every interaction, micro-animation, and visual effect should serve as direct proof of frontend mastery.
2. **Frictionless Navigation**: The visitor should easily flow between high-level project overviews and deep architectural deep-dives without getting lost.
3. **Impeccable Craft**: The polish is the product. No broken links, unoptimized mobile views, or jarring transitions.
